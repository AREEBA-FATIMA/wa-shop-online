# Direction Contract — WA-SHOP

Variant: redesign-existing-projects → minimalist-ui (product UI)
Dials: VARIANCE 2 · MOTION 2 · DENSITY 7

Design language (one line): an authentic WhatsApp-Business feel — real WhatsApp
greens and teals, chat-app surfaces, calm and trustworthy, mobile-first.

References: WhatsApp Business app, Linear (density/rhythm), Stripe dashboard (trust).

Type: Plus Jakarta Sans (headings) + Inter (body). Tighten heading tracking.

Palette intent: #25D366 primary action; #008069/#00a884 WhatsApp teal for brand
surfaces; authentic chat wallpaper + bubble colors; retire decorative purple.

Motion intent: short, ease-out, functional only. No bounce, no float-on-load spam.

Do NOT:
- purple/indigo gradients or "AI-slop" multi-stop decorative gradients
- bounce/elastic easing; motion longer than 400ms
- cramped padding or <44px touch targets
- more than one accent hue competing with WhatsApp green/teal
- inconsistent theme storage (unify on one key, no flash)
